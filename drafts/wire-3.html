Questions.

Do I have to type the scenes in myself?

You can, and on some productions that is the right call. Add scenes and characters by hand and build the breakdown as you go. On Pro and Enterprise plans you can import the script instead, and the scenes, characters and who is in what come out ready to work on.

What happens when a new draft comes in?

StageRoll reads it and shows you what changed: scenes added, moved or omitted, characters renamed. Nothing on your production moves until you approve it, and every department’s notes and photographs stay on the scenes they belong to.

Can my whole department use it?

Yes. Invite anyone by email. Coordinators run the department, crew check things in and out and take photographs, and the owner sees the whole production. Costume, props, hair and makeup each see the same production through their own lens.

Where do my continuity photographs go?

Take them in the app or add them from your camera roll. Each one is filed to its scene and to what it shows, whether that is a change, a style, a look or a prop, so the gallery runs in script order or by shoot day rather than by whatever you photographed last.

Does it replace my paperwork?

Costume plots, continuity books, bag tags, props by scene, wrap boxes and more come out as PDF or CSV from what is already in the production. Run them again after a redraft in one tap.

What languages are supported?

English, for now. The app and its reports are in English, and script reading is built for English-language scripts. Scripts in other languages may not read as expected.

Who can see my production?

Only the people you have invited. Take someone off the production and their access goes with them. Your scripts, photographs and notes are never sold, shown to advertisers or used to train anything.
